# Sources and Scope

Links checked 14 November 2026. Dates describe the source, not new research. Recheck live pages when the answer depends on current facts. This is an original educational workflow, not an official WSAVA/AAHA skill or an independently clinically validated tool. Link to copyrighted charts rather than reproducing them.

AAHA describes formal peer review, expert-consensus components and disclosed industry educational funding. Do not equate a respected source with certainty for every claim or an endorsement of its sponsors.

## Core nutrition references

- [WSAVA Global Nutrition Toolkit](https://wsava.org/global-guidelines/global-nutrition-guidelines/): body/muscle condition, diet history and owner resources.
- [WSAVA selecting pet foods, updated 10 March 2021](https://wsava.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Selecting-a-pet-food-for-your-pet-updated-2021_WSAVA-Global-Nutrition-Toolkit.pdf): adequacy statements, life-stage fit, energy, formulation expertise, manufacturing quality and research.
- [WSAVA dog muscle-condition chart](https://wsava.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Muscle-Condition-Score-Chart-for-Dogs.pdf): normal muscle mass and mild, moderate or severe loss; separate from body fat.
- [AAHA 2021 screening evaluation](https://www.aaha.org/resources/2021-aaha-nutrition-and-weight-management-guidelines/screening-evaluation/): diet, environment, activity and physical assessment.
- [AAHA 2021 nutritional risk factors](https://www.aaha.org/resources/2021-aaha-nutrition-and-weight-management-guidelines/nutritional-risk-factors/): reasons for extended evaluation rather than generic feeding changes.
- [AAHA 2021 healthy feeding plans](https://www.aaha.org/resources/2021-aaha-nutrition-and-weight-management-guidelines/feeding-plans-for-healthy-appropriate-weight-cats-and-dogs/): complete-food/extras balance, individualized feeding and monitoring.
- [AAHA energy calculation table](https://www.aaha.org/wp-content/uploads/globalassets/02-guidelines/2021-nutrition-and-weight-management/resourcepdfs/nutritiongl_box1.pdf): RER, maintenance factors and individual adjustment. Use only the scope explained in calculation-checks.md.
- [AAHA full 2021 guideline PDF](https://www.aaha.org/wp-content/uploads/globalassets/02-guidelines/2021-nutrition-and-weight-management/resourcepdfs/new-2021-aaha-nutrition-and-weight-management-guidelines-with-ref.pdf): official alternative if an individual AAHA page fails to open.

## Supporting references

- [AAFCO consumer FAQ](https://www.aafco.org/consumers/understanding-pet-food/frequently-asked-questions/): US adequacy statements and the distinction between standards and certification. AAFCO does not approve/certify individual foods. Do not export US rules to other jurisdictions.
- [Merck Veterinary Manual: when to see a veterinarian](https://www.merckvetmanual.com/multimedia/table/when-to-see-a-veterinarian): urgent owner-facing signs. Use local veterinary services; verify contacts rather than assuming US services apply.
- [Canine pancreatitis feeding study, Mansfield et al. 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21418318/) and [Harris et al. 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28510290/): limited primary evidence supporting early enteral feeding in clinical settings. These are not at-home tube-feeding instructions or proof that every vomiting dog should immediately be fed. Avoid a blanket fasting rule; refer the actual case to its veterinarian.

Use manufacturer labels only for product facts. Do not treat testimonials, skill marketplaces, this source list or another AI's output as clinical evidence.
